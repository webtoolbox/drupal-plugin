# Website Toolbox Forum — Drupal Forum Module

Add a powerful, fully hosted **community forum to your Drupal website** in minutes. The Website Toolbox Forum module for Drupal gives you an embeddable discussion board, single sign-on (SSO) with your Drupal users, and automatic member and role synchronization — with no database, server, or coding to maintain.

Website Toolbox is the easiest way to add a forum, message board, or online community to Drupal. Ranked the #1 forum provider by StarReviews and trusted by more than 2,000 creators and businesses.

## Choose the module folder for your Drupal version

Each Drupal release line has its own directory in this repository:

- **[`drupal7.x/`](drupal7.x)** — Website Toolbox Forum for Drupal 7
- **[`drupal8.x-9.x/`](drupal8.x-9.x)** — Website Toolbox Forum for Drupal 8 and Drupal 9
- **[`drupal10.x/`](drupal10.x)** — Website Toolbox Forum for Drupal 10

Download the folder that matches your Drupal version and install it as the `website_toolbox_forum` module (see [Installation](#installation) below). When you upgrade Drupal, switch to the folder for your new version.

## Features

- **Instant forum setup** — add a discussion forum to Drupal with no server or database to maintain.
- **Embedded forum or standalone page** — embed the discussion board directly into a Drupal page, or link to your forum from the site navigation.
- **Single Sign On (SSO)** — Drupal users are signed in to the forum automatically, and signed out when they leave Drupal.
- **Signup integration** — new Drupal accounts automatically create matching forum members.
- **Role synchronization** — Drupal roles map to forum user groups, so member permissions stay in sync.
- **SEO and mobile friendly** — fast, responsive, search-engine-optimized forum pages that grow organic traffic.
- **Secure and private** — public or private forums, anti-spam protection, and automatic AI moderation.
- **Your own branding** — match the forum to your Drupal site with themes, colors, and custom layouts.

## Installation

1. Download this repository, or just the module folder for your Drupal version.
2. Copy the matching folder into your Drupal site's custom modules directory and rename it to `website_toolbox_forum`:
   - Drupal 8 / 9 / 10 — `modules/custom/website_toolbox_forum`
   - Drupal 7 — `sites/all/modules/website_toolbox_forum`
3. Enable the **Website Toolbox Forum** module:
   - Drupal 8 / 9 / 10 — **Extend** (`/admin/modules`)
   - Drupal 7 — **Modules** (`/admin/modules`)
4. Open **Configuration → Website Toolbox Forum**, enter your Website Toolbox forum username and password, choose whether the forum is embedded, and save. A **Forum** link then appears in your site navigation.

Full step-by-step walkthrough: [Drupal forum module](https://www.websitetoolbox.com/blog/drupal-forum-module-829).
